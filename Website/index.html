Reactive Robot

Human-Aware Trajectory Replanning for Safe Robotic Operations

A next-generation robotic arm system with real-time obstacle detection and proactive safety mechanisms for industrial environments.

arrow_downward Learn More

Project Overview

This capstone project addresses the design and implementation of a robotic arm capable of real-time, human- and obstacle-aware trajectory replanning.

Conventional industrial robotic arms typically depend on passive safety mechanisms, such as safety cages or emergency stop systems, which activate only after contact has occurred. This approach can lead to safety risks and reduced productivity.

Our solution incorporates proactive sensing and control strategies, using a servo-driven robotic arm with a stepper-motor–based rotating base, along with infrared (IR) and optical sensors to detect obstacles before contact.
